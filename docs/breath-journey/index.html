Breathing is not a switch

Breathing is the one part of the autonomic nervous system you can take hold of directly. That is genuinely remarkable, and it is why every tradition that works with bodies has found its way to the breath.

It has also made the breath the most oversold thing in the field. Somewhere between the real physiology and the workshop version, “slow down and breathe out for longer” turned into switching your nervous system between states, and the second one is not something anyone can currently support.

This is a reading journey through what is established, in a series of short stops and about half an hour. Every figure can be operated. Every claim names its source before you meet it, and says how much that source can carry.

The good news arrives early and it is better than the oversold version: there really is a rate at which the heart and the blood vessels and the breath fall into step, it is findable, and it is about six breaths a minute. Most of this page is that, and what it is not.

Who it is for

Massage therapists, bodyworkers, physiotherapists, nurses, yoga and movement teachers, somatic practitioners — anyone who has ever said and now just breathe to a person on a table, and wondered afterwards exactly what they were claiming.

No science background needed. Where a technical word is the right one, we introduce it and say what it means in ordinary language beside it.

What is here

One · what breathing is doing. What an ordinary rate looks like, why the lungs are not where the interesting part happens, and what goes wrong when you breathe more than the body asked for.

Two · why slow works. The heart is already following your breath. One rate makes that swing largest, for a reason that has a decent account behind it — and that rate is not the same for everybody.

Three · being honest about it. What breathwork does in randomised trials, which is real and modest, and how much weight each thing here can carry.

What we carry back. What we came away with, and every source in the order the journey met them.

Nothing here is medical advice. Anyone with a heart or lung condition, or who is pregnant, should take breathing exercises up with a clinician rather than with a web page.

Before you start If you take one thing from this: slower is the active ingredient, not deeper. A great deal of what follows is that sentence, with the evidence attached — and one stop about what deeper on its own can actually do.
